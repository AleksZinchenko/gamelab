Game art style sheet for an ORIGINAL side-scrolling 2D platformer about Christmas Eve in a small snowy European town with half-timbered (fachwerk) houses. One sheet on a plain flat background, neatly arranged with space between elements, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no labels.
Contents:
1) The hero: a cheerful kid about 10 years old in a red knitted hat with a pompom, a long striped scarf, a warm coat, mittens and boots, with a wooden slingshot. Shown three times: standing idle facing right, running, aiming the slingshot.
2) Two villains from a gang that hates holidays, comical not scary: a skinny sneaky thief in a grey scarf and flat cap carrying a sack of stolen presents; a chubby grumpy bully in a grey coat holding a lump of coal.
3) One two-storey half-timbered house facade: dark timber frame on white plaster, steep snowy roof, chimney, warm lit windows, a wreath on the door.
4) A row of small collectible items: candy cane, gingerbread man, wrapped candy, glass Christmas bauble, golden star, small gift box with ribbon, pine cone (slingshot ammo), paper party firecracker with a spark.
5) A strip of snowy cobblestone ground tiles and an old street lamp.
6) A row of palette swatch squares along the bottom edge.
STYLE: classic 8-bit console pixel art. Very low resolution: the hero is about 16 pixels tall, tiles are 16x16 pixels, every pixel is a hard square of one flat colour, magnified with nearest-neighbour so the big pixels are clearly visible. Strict limited palette of about 13 colours, at most 3 colours per sprite plus a black 1-pixel outline. No gradients, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft shading. Background: flat deep navy night.
