STYLE: minimalist handheld-console pixel art with a strict 4-tone palette of cold blues (#0f1a2e, #3b5b8c, #8fb3d9, #e8f1fa) plus ONE accent colour, festive red (#d6283a), used only for the hero's hat and scarf, the gift box, the bauble and the candy cane. Golden star and lit windows are drawn in the lightest tone. The hero is about 24 pixels tall, big clearly visible pixels, flat fills, no gradients, no anti-aliasing. Background: the lightest tone.
