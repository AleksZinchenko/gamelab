Use case: sheet of rooftop sections for a side-scrolling platformer level that is played ON the roofs of a snowy half-timbered town (the hero runs and jumps along roof ridges, flat roof tops and slopes).
Image 2 shows the house design to follow (timber pattern, windows, roof, snow, outline).
Subject: 4 separate rooftop sections, each one = the TOP storey of a half-timbered (fachwerk) house plus its roof, in strict front-on side view, cut off with a straight horizontal edge at the bottom of the storey (the wall continues below in the game): (1) a steep symmetric gable roof with a small dormer, a brick chimney on one side; (2) a wide roof with a long FLAT snowy top (a mansard: short steep sides, wide flat top) and two dormer windows; (3) a single long gentle slope (lean-to roof) rising from left to right, with a skylight; (4) a narrow tower-like house top with a small flat roof terrace surrounded by a low wooden railing. Dark timber frames on white plaster, flat amber lit windows, thick snow lying on every roof top and edge, small icicles on the eaves.
Composition: the four sections side by side in one row, all with their flat bottom edges on one line at 96% height, wide empty gaps (at least 6% of the width) between them; similar storey heights; each section complete inside the frame.
Constraints: background flat solid #FF00FF; no ground, no street, no lamps, no people, no birds, no text.
