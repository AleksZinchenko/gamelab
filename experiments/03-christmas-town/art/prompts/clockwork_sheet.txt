Use case: sheet of clockwork pieces for a vertical side-view platformer level inside a clock tower (the hero jumps on gears and beams).
Subject: 7 separate pieces in strict front view: (1) a BIG brass gear wheel seen straight on as a perfect circle with chunky square teeth all around, spokes and a dark iron hub; (2) a MEDIUM steel-grey gear, the same style; (3) a SMALL copper gear, the same style; (4) a long clock pendulum: a thin iron rod hanging from a pivot at the top with a big round brass bob at the bottom; (5) a straight horizontal heavy wooden beam with iron bands, square ends, that can be repeated sideways; (6) a short wooden platform shelf on a black iron bracket fixed to a wall; (7) a small wooden crate of coal (black lumps on top).
Composition: two rows, every piece centred in its own area with wide empty gaps; the big gear about twice the diameter of the medium one; the pendulum tall and vertical.
Constraints: background flat solid #FF00FF; no wall, no background, no people, no text, no numbers.
