Use case: the big town Christmas tree that stands in the middle of the market square of a side-scrolling platformer; the game hangs ornaments, garlands and a star on it in code, so the tree itself must be BARE.
Subject: one tall, full, symmetric fir tree in strict side view, completely undecorated: no baubles, no lights, no garland, no star, no ribbon. Six clear stacked tiers of drooping dark-green branches with snow lying on top of every tier, branch tips pointing slightly down, small gaps of darker shadow between tiers so ornaments can hang under each tier. The trunk shows only a little at the bottom and stands in a low round stone planter ring with a snowy rim. The very top is a single thin bare leader shoot (where a star will be placed later).
Composition: the tree centred, filling 90% of the image height, base of the planter at 95% height; the widest bottom tier about 75% of the image width.
Constraints: background flat solid #FF00FF; no ground strip, no houses, no people, no text, no decorations of any kind.
