Use case: sheet of small rooftop props for a side-scrolling platformer level played on snowy roofs.
Subject: 8 separate props in strict side view: (1) a tall free-standing brick chimney with a snowy cap and a dark opening, no smoke; (2) a short wide brick chimney with two clay pots; (3) a wrought-iron weather vane with a rooster on top; (4) a straight vertical wooden ladder segment, two side rails and four rungs, that can be repeated vertically (top and bottom cut straight); (5) a big soft snowdrift heap; (6) a short wooden plank bridge (three planks bound with rope), seen from the side; (7) a flat slab of snow broken off a roof, chunky, with a blue shadow underside; (8) a wooden crate with a snowy top and a rope handle.
Composition: two rows of four, every prop centred in its own area with wide empty gaps; the tall chimney about twice the height of the crate; the ladder segment about as tall as the crate.
Constraints: background flat solid #FF00FF; no ground strip, no smoke, no people, no birds, no text.
